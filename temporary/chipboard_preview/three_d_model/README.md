Similar-product modern chipboard/plywood screw visualization; not an exact Screw-Tite model or certified DIN part.

Dimensions and assumptions:

- identity: Modern chipboard/plywood screw proxy requested by user; slim shank with 40 degree wood threads. Screw-Tite remains the preferred comparison; its exact Tri-Lock, ribs and twin-start transition are not reproduced.
- head: HSB nominal/midpoint head diameter and published head/neck clearance. Selected 90 degree cone, 0.10d rim, small fillet and a slender cylindrical neck; head-to-thread clearance is shortened only on tiny catalogue extensions.
- thread: HSB modern chipboard core midpoints and nominal pitch, 40 degree flanks, selected 0.02P crest land. Plain shank uses published minimum diameter, independently of the thread crest.
- thread_coverage: Full thread through max(25 mm, 6d), otherwise b=0.6L plus one-pitch runout. Selected coverage proxy guided by the preferred Screw-Tite short/long range; not a claim of HSB stock coverage.
- runout: Selected one-pitch fade into a smooth shank; limited to one third of the threaded envelope on very short entries.
- tip: Selected point length 1.25 diameters, shortened to 0.6 of available shaft on short extensions. This approximates a plain sharp chipboard point; cutting flutes and serrations omitted.
- display: Smooth pointed thread envelope tapering into the slender plain shank; same recessed head.
- recess: 3/3.5/4 mm use Fabory DIN 7997 Z mouth and minimum depth. Other diameters explicitly borrow Bossard DIN 965 Z mouth and midpoint depth. ISO 4757 wing/taper visual cavity; not a gauge model.
- certification: Similar-product visualization only; does not identify the stocked product or certify DIN conformity.
- catalogue_length: Conservative extension label for every length; comparable dimension tables do not establish Screw-Tite stock availability.
- small_sizes: 2/2.5 mm modern head, core, slender shank and neck are scaled from the sourced 3 mm chipboard profile. Pitch uses the published small wood-thread table; existing similar-head PZ1 cavity retained. Explicit dimensional extensions, not supplier measurements.
- markings: Unidentified manufacturer and property-class stamps omitted.
